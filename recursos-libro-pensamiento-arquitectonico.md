# Recursos para "Pensamiento Arquitectonico: Principios Atemporales para Disenar Software"

Recopilacion exhaustiva de fuentes, referencias y recursos para el libro.

## 1. Libros Fundamentales (Fuentes Primarias)

### "A Philosophy of Software Design" - John Ousterhout
- **Autor:** John Ousterhout (Stanford University)
- **Edicion:** 2nd Edition, 2018/2021
- **URL sitio oficial:** https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/book.php
- **Review detallado (Pragmatic Engineer):** https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/a-philosophy-of-software-design-review/
- **Resumen detallado (Carsten Behrens):** https://carstenbehrens.com/a-philosophy-of-software-design-summary/
- **Resumen visual (Matt Duck):** https://www.mattduck.com/2021-04-a-philosophy-of-software-design.html
- **Resumen (Jan Meppe):** https://www.janmeppe.com/blog/a-philosophy-of-software-design-john-ousterhout/
- **Extracto PDF del autor:** https://web.stanford.edu/~ouster/cgi-bin/aposd2ndEdExtract.pdf
- **Aporte al libro:** Tesis central sobre complejidad como enemigo principal del diseno. Conceptos de modulos profundos vs. superficiales, programacion estrategica vs. tactica, y la critica a TDD como metodologia de diseno.

### "Clean Code" y "Clean Architecture" - Robert C. Martin
- **Clean Code (2nd Ed, 2025):** https://www.amazon.com/Clean-Code-Handbook-Software-Craftsmanship/dp/0135398576
- **Clean Architecture:** https://www.amazon.com/Clean-Architecture-Craftsmans-Software-Structure/dp/0134494164
- **Critica exhaustiva de Clean Code 2nd Ed (Bugzmanov, 2025):** https://bugzmanov.github.io/cleancode-critique/clean_code_second_edition_review.html
- **Critica de Clean Code 1st Ed (Bugzmanov):** https://bugzmanov.github.io/cleancode-critique/
- **"Why I Can't Recommend Clean Architecture" (DEV):** https://dev.to/bosepchuk/why-i-cant-recommend-clean-architecture-by-robert-c-martin-ofd
- **Discusion en Hacker News sobre Clean Architecture:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=16058979
- **Debate Ousterhout vs. Martin (GitHub):** https://github.com/johnousterhout/aposd-vs-clean-code
- **Debate en Hacker News (Clean Code vs APoSD):** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43166362
- **Comparacion Mozaic Works:** https://mozaicworks.com/blog/clean-code-vs-a-philosophy-of-software-design
- **Aporte al libro:** Contraste entre dos filosofias de diseno. Martin prioriza funciones pequenas y decomposicion agresiva; Ousterhout prioriza reducir carga cognitiva total. Esencial para el capitulo sobre el debate moderno del diseno de software.

### "Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable OO Software" (GoF)
- **Autores:** Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, John Vlissides (1994)
- **Analisis de relevancia 2025 (Medium):** https://medium.com/@freddy.dordoni/the-gang-of-four-gave-us-23-design-patterns-are-they-still-relevant-in-2025-f2e999c384c0
- **Patrones revisitados para juegos:** https://gameprogrammingpatterns.com/design-patterns-revisited.html
- **Patrones en la era de IA:** https://dev.to/tawe/design-patterns-after-the-singularity-rethinking-the-gang-of-four-for-an-ai-driven-stack-462e
- **Relevancia hoy (Mario Cervera):** https://mariocervera.com/are-design-patterns-still-relevant
- **"Bad Smells of GoF Design Patterns" (paper academico, MDPI):** https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/13/18/10256
- **"Don't Read Design Patterns" (Medium):** https://medium.com/@jaustinjr.blog/dont-read-design-patterns-elements-of-reusable-object-oriented-software-here-s-why-abd712a73358
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_Patterns
- **Aporte al libro:** Analisis critico de la vigencia de los 23 patrones clasicos. Muchos estan integrados en lenguajes modernos; otros siguen siendo esenciales en arquitecturas de microservicios y sistemas event-driven.

### "Thinking in Systems: A Primer" - Donella Meadows
- **Amazon:** https://www.amazon.com/Thinking-Systems-Donella-H-Meadows/dp/1603580557
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_In_Systems:_A_Primer
- **Recursos de pensamiento sistemico (Donella Meadows Project):** https://donellameadows.org/systems-thinking-resources/
- **Review tecnica (beza1e1):** https://beza1e1.tuxen.de/thinking_in_systems.html
- **Review Eric Nehrlich:** https://www.nehrlich.com/blog/2021/01/07/thinking-in-systems-by-donella-meadows/
- **Discusion en HN:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=12210629
- **"Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System" (ensayo completo):** https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/
- **PDF del ensayo Leverage Points:** https://donellameadows.org/wp-content/userfiles/Leverage_Points.pdf
- **Guia aplicada a workflows (Kanban Zone, 2025):** https://kanbanzone.com/2025/thinking-in-systems-a-guide-to-smarter-workflows/
- **Aporte al libro:** Marco conceptual de stocks, flujos, retroalimentacion y puntos de apalancamiento aplicado a sistemas de software. Los 12 leverage points son directamente aplicables a decisiones arquitectonicas.

### "How to Solve It" - George Polya
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_to_Solve_It
- **Internet Archive (libro completo):** https://archive.org/details/polya-how-to-solve-it
- **Aplicacion a software (DEV Community):** https://dev.to/lovestaco/solving-software-problems-with-common-sense-lessons-from-polya-mfm
- **Polya para programadores (DEV):** https://dev.to/shlokaguptaa/george-polyas-how-to-solve-it-hh7
- **Polya y problem solving en codigo (GitHub gist):** https://gist.github.com/jph00/d60301884c56fe063101a7cc6193b3af
- **Review para programadores:** http://timothypratley.blogspot.com/2013/12/how-to-solve-it-book-review.html
- **Polya como matematico y design thinking:** https://alliwalk.com/blog/2019/09/solving-design-problems-like-a-mathematician/
- **Polya tecnicas de problem solving (DEV):** https://dev.to/arthurlch/polyas-problem-solving-techniques-23nc
- **Aporte al libro:** Los 4 pasos de Polya (entender, planificar, ejecutar, reflexionar) son directamente mapeables al proceso de diseno de software. El "looking back" es el equivalente a la retrospectiva arquitectonica.

### "Software Design X-Rays" - Adam Tornhill
- **Pragmatic Programmers:** https://pragprog.com/titles/atevol/software-design-x-rays/
- **Sitio del autor:** https://www.adamtornhill.com/articles/swevorelease.html
- **Key Points (Understand Legacy Code):** https://understandlegacycode.com/blog/key-points-of-software-design-x-rays/
- **Review (Lassi Autio):** https://lassiautio.com/2018/09/15/software-design-x-rays-by-adam-tornhill/
- **"Your Code as a Crime Scene" (2nd ed, 2024):** https://pragprog.com/titles/atcrime2/your-code-as-a-crime-scene-second-edition/
- **Tech Lead Journal entrevista:** https://techleadjournal.dev/episodes/241/
- **USENIX talk:** https://www.usenix.org/conference/srecon24emea/presentation/tornhill
- **Aporte al libro:** Analisis conductual del codigo usando datos del sistema de control de versiones. Hotspots, change coupling, y la dimension social del diseno de software.

### "Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs" (SICP)
- **Autores:** Harold Abelson, Gerald Jay Sussman, Julie Sussman
- **PDF MIT:** https://web.mit.edu/6.001/6.037/sicp.pdf
- **Edicion HTML:** https://sarabander.github.io/sicp/html/index.xhtml
- **Edicion JavaScript:** https://sicp.sourceacademy.org/sicpjs.pdf
- **MIT Press:** https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262543231/structure-and-interpretation-of-computer-programs/
- **"Why SICP Matters" (Brian Harvey, Berkeley):** https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~bh/sicp.html
- **Review de Tornhill sobre SICP:** https://www.adamtornhill.com/reviews/sicp.htm
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_and_Interpretation_of_Computer_Programs
- **Aporte al libro:** Fundamento teorico de la abstraccion como herramienta central del diseno. Funciones como datos, meta-programacion, y el pensamiento computacional como forma de razonamiento sobre sistemas complejos.

### "The Pragmatic Programmer" - Andrew Hunt y David Thomas
- **Tips oficiales:** https://pragprog.com/tips/
- **Resumen de principios (Jeff Bailey):** https://jeffbailey.us/blog/2025/10/08/a-list-of-pragmatic-programmer-principles/
- **Takeaways (Arkadiusz Chmura):** https://arkadiuszchmura.com/posts/my-key-takeaways-from-the-pragmatic-programmer/
- **Resumen GitHub:** https://github.com/HugoMatilla/The-Pragmatic-Programmer
- **Quick Reference:** https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/home/lieber/courses/csg110/sp08/Pragmatic%20Quick%20Reference.htm
- **Lecciones en la era de IA (Medium):** https://medium.com/@cangokdere/pragmatic-programmer-lessons-in-the-age-of-ai-614d2a144c78
- **Aporte al libro:** Principios DRY, ETC (Easier to Change), Design by Contract, orthogonality, tracer bullets. Filosofia pragmatica versus dogmatica del diseno.

## 2. Papers y Articulos Academicos

### Cohesion y Acoplamiento - Larry Constantine y Edward Yourdon
- **"Structured Design" (Stevens, Myers, Constantine, 1974):** IBM Systems Journal, Vol. 13, No. 2, May 1974
- **Libro "Structured Design" (Yourdon & Constantine, 1979):** https://archive.org/details/Structured_Design_Edward_Yourdon_Larry_Constantine
- **Texto completo en Archive.org:** https://archive.org/stream/Structured_Design_Edward_Yourdon_Larry_Constantine/Structured_Design_Edward_Yourdon_Larry_Constantine_djvu.txt
- **Citas del libro:** https://wstomv.win.tue.nl/quotes/structured-design.html
- **Larry Constantine Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_Constantine
- **Historia de cohesion y acoplamiento:** https://mrpicky.dev/a-brief-history-of-coupling-and-cohesion/
- **Fecha original:** Conceptos desarrollados ~1968, publicados formalmente en 1974
- **Aporte al libro:** Fuente primaria de los conceptos de cohesion y acoplamiento. Constantine desarrollo estos conceptos a mediados de los 60s, primera publicacion en 1968.

### Principio de Sustitucion - Barbara Liskov
- **"Data Abstraction and Hierarchy" (Liskov, 1987):** Keynote, OOPSLA 1987
- **"A Behavioral Notion of Subtyping" (Liskov & Wing, 1994):** ACM TOPLAS, Vol. 16, No. 6, pp. 1811-1841
  - ACM Digital Library: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/197320.197383
  - PDF MIT: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~wing/publications/LiskovWing94.pdf
  - ResearchGate: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/2463573_A_Behavioral_Notion_of_Subtyping
  - Semantic Scholar: https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/A-behavioral-notion-of-subtyping-Liskov-Wing/768984602be8e1531fe300a3c590bbf9b37a3186
- **Wikipedia LSP:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liskov_substitution_principle
- **Aporte al libro:** Definicion formal del principio de sustitucion y su formalizacion con logica de Hoare. Conexion con Design by Contract de Bertrand Meyer.

### SOLID - Robert C. Martin
- **"Design Principles and Design Patterns" (Martin, 2000):** Paper original que introduce los principios
  - Academia.edu: https://www.academia.edu/40543946/Design_Principles_and_Design_Patterns
  - DocPlayer: https://docplayer.net/346979-Design-principles-and-design-patterns.html
- **Principles Wiki - Coleccion de Martin:** http://principles-wiki.net/collections:robert_c._martin_s_principle_collection
- **SOLID Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOLID
- **Goodreads - Serie de papers:** https://www.goodreads.com/series/160070-object-mentor-solid-design-papers
- **Nota:** El acronimo SOLID fue acunado por Michael Feathers ~2004
- **Aporte al libro:** Fuente primaria de los 5 principios SOLID. El paper de 2000 introduce los conceptos de "software rot" y los principios como antidoto.

### Complejidad de Software - McCabe y Halstead
- **Thomas McCabe, "A Complexity Measure" (1976):** IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering
  - Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclomatic_complexity
  - NIST paper: https://www.mccabe.com/pdf/mccabe-nist235r.pdf
  - Critica academica: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/3407068_A_Critique_of_Cyclomatic_Complexity_as_a_Software_Metric
- **Maurice Halstead, "Elements of Software Science" (1977)**
  - Metricas explicadas: https://blog.codacy.com/code-complexity
  - Comparacion comprensiva de metricas: https://thevaluable.dev/complexity-metrics-software/
- **Estudio comparativo (Springer):** https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-75381-0_15
- **Complementariedad de metricas (ScienceDirect, 2025):** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0164121225003486
- **Aporte al libro:** Metricas formales para medir complejidad. McCabe: complejidad ciclomatica (caminos independientes). Halstead: volumen, dificultad, esfuerzo basado en operadores/operandos.

### Information Hiding - David Parnas
- **"On the Criteria To Be Used in Decomposing Systems into Modules" (Parnas, 1972)**
  - ACM Digital Library: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/361598.361623
  - MIT: http://sunnyday.mit.edu/16.355/parnas-criteria.html
  - ResearchGate: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/200085877_On_the_Criteria_To_Be_Used_in_Decomposing_Systems_into_Modules
  - Semantic Scholar: https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/On-the-criteria-to-be-used-in-decomposing-systems-Parnas/877e314d3a9f9317c162309c9ee0c660878a4bdb
- **The Morning Paper analisis:** https://blog.acolyer.org/2016/09/05/on-the-criteria-to-be-used-in-decomposing-systems-into-modules/
- **Eric Normand podcast sobre el paper:** https://ericnormand.me/podcast/on-the-criteria-to-be-used-in-decomposing-systems-into-modules
- **Analisis anotado (Ole Miss, Conrad Cunningham):** https://john.cs.olemiss.edu/~hcc//researchMethods/2016spr/notes/ClassicParnas/IEEEannotated/ClassicParnasRevisionAnnotated.pdf
- **Understanding Parnas (Medium):** https://mendelbak.medium.com/understanding-david-parnas-information-hiding-and-system-modularization-f491420d2d87
- **Wikipedia David Parnas:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Parnas
- **Aporte al libro:** Paper fundacional del information hiding. El ejemplo del indice KWIC demuestra dos descomposiciones del mismo sistema: una basada en flujo de datos y otra basada en decisiones de diseno que pueden cambiar.

### Papers Adicionales Fundamentales

#### Fred Brooks - "No Silver Bullet" (1986)
- **Wikipedia:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Silver_Bullet
- **PDF UNC:** https://www.cs.unc.edu/techreports/86-020.pdf
- **PDF (Bret Victor):** https://worrydream.com/refs/Brooks_1986_-_No_Silver_Bullet.pdf

- **ACM Digital Library:** https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/362929.362947
